About Us

Welcome to CalcConvert — a simple and accessible platform designed to make everyday calculations and conversions easier for everyone.

Our goal is to provide useful online calculators and converters that help individuals, students, professionals and businesses save time and get quick results without complicated processes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make calculations simple, practical and accessible. We aim to develop easy-to-use online tools that provide clear results and help users make better-informed decisions in their daily activities.

What We Offer

CalcConvert is being developed as a growing collection of online tools across different categories, including:

  • General Calculators: Percentage, discount, date and other everyday calculations.
  • Finance & Money: Salary, tax and other financial calculators.
  • Business Calculators: Practical tools for business and workplace calculations.
  • Unit Converters: Tools for converting common units and measurements.
  • Education: Learning-focused calculators and useful educational tools.

We plan to expand our collection over time and introduce more useful tools based on users' needs.

Our Approach

We believe online tools should be straightforward and easy to use. We focus on simple interfaces, practical functionality and clear results.

We also aim to review and improve our tools regularly. While we make reasonable efforts to provide accurate results, users should verify important financial, tax or other professional calculations with appropriate official sources.

Who Can Use CalcConvert?

Our tools are intended for a wide range of users, including students, employees, freelancers, professionals and business owners. Whether you need a quick percentage calculation or a practical financial tool, CalcConvert aims to make the process easier.
