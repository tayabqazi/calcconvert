About the Date Calculator

CalcConvert's free Date Calculator helps you calculate the difference between two dates, count individual weekdays, identify working days and weekends, and find future or past dates by adding or subtracting time.

How to Use the Date Calculator

  • Date Difference: Select a start date and an end date, then click Calculate.
  • Weekday Counter: The same selected dates automatically calculate the number of Sundays, Mondays and all other weekdays.
  • Working Days: View the total Monday-to-Friday days and Saturday-to-Sunday weekend days.
  • Add or Subtract: Select a starting date, choose Add or Subtract, enter a number and select days, weeks or months.

Calculation Examples

Example 1: Count Sundays
Select October 1 to October 30. The calculator will show the exact number of Sundays and every other weekday in that range.
Example 2: Working Days
Select any two dates to find total weekdays (Monday to Friday) and weekend days (Saturday and Sunday).
Example 3: Add Days
If your starting date is January 1 and you add 30 days, the calculator will show the resulting date.

Calculation Methods

Date Difference: The calculator finds the calendar difference between the selected dates. The total day count is also shown.

Weekday Count: Each calendar date from the start date through the end date is counted, including both selected dates.

Working Days: Monday through Friday are counted as working days. Saturday and Sunday are counted as weekend days.

Add / Subtract: Days and weeks are calculated as calendar days. When adding or subtracting months, the day of the month is adjusted to the last valid day of the destination month when necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I count how many Sundays are between two dates?

Yes. Enter the start and end dates in the Date Difference Calculator. It will show the exact count for Sunday and every other day of the week.

Are the start and end dates included?

Yes. Both dates are included in the weekday and working-day count.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses calendar dates and automatically accounts for leap years, including February 29.

Does the working-day calculation exclude public holidays?

No. The working-day count includes Monday to Friday and does not automatically exclude public holidays. It also does not account for company-specific working schedules.

Can I save the calculation as a PDF?

Yes. Click Print / Save as PDF and select Save as PDF in your browser's print options.

Is this Date Calculator free?

Yes. All date calculation tools are free to use.
