About the Monthly Payroll Calculator

This free Monthly Payroll Calculator helps employers, HR teams, payroll professionals and employees calculate monthly salary and net payable amounts. It includes basic salary, allowances, attendance deductions, employee contributions and employer PF.

You can enter different earnings and deductions according to your organisation's payroll policy. The calculator updates the payroll summary automatically as you enter the figures.

How to Calculate Monthly Payroll

  • Enter employee information and the relevant pay month.
  • Enter basic salary and applicable allowances.
  • Enter absent days and salary divisor, if applicable.
  • Enter other deductions such as PF, EOBI, income tax, advances and loans.
  • Enter employer PF separately, if applicable.
  • Review the gross salary, total deductions and net payable salary in the payroll summary.

Payroll Calculation Formulas

Gross Salary = Basic Salary + Total Allowances
Absent Deduction = (Basic Salary ÷ Salary Divisor) × Absent Days
Total Deductions = Absent Deduction + Other Applicable Deductions
Net Payable Salary = Gross Salary − Total Deductions
Employer PF = Employer Contribution (shown separately)

Payroll Calculation Example

Suppose an employee has a basic salary of 60,000 and total allowances of 15,000. Gross salary will be 75,000. If the employee has one absent day and the salary divisor is 30, the absent deduction will be 2,000. Any additional deductions are then subtracted to calculate net payable salary.

This calculator provides general payroll calculations. Tax, PF, EOBI, attendance and other deductions should be entered according to applicable laws and your organisation's payroll policy. The calculator does not automatically determine statutory deduction rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a Monthly Payroll Calculator?

A Monthly Payroll Calculator helps calculate employee gross salary, allowances, deductions and net payable salary for a selected pay period.

2. How is gross salary calculated?

Gross salary is calculated by adding basic salary and all applicable earnings and allowances.

3. How is absent deduction calculated?

Absent deduction is calculated by dividing basic salary by the salary divisor and multiplying the result by the number of absent days.

4. Can I include different allowances?

Yes. You can enter house rent, medical, vehicle, fuel, TA/DA, OPD, mobile, bonus, commission and other allowances.

5. Is employer PF deducted from net salary?

No. Employer PF contribution is displayed separately and is not included in employee deductions.

6. Does this calculator automatically calculate income tax and EOBI?

No. Income tax, EOBI, PF and other deductions are entered manually. Please use the applicable rates and rules for your payroll.

7. Can I print or save the payroll summary?

Yes. Use the Print / Save as PDF button to print the payroll summary or save it as a PDF file.
