About the Age Calculator

CalcConvert's Advanced Age Calculator helps you calculate your exact age between two dates. You can also add multiple people and compare their age differences.

How to Use

  • Enter the date of birth for the first person.
  • Select the date up to which you want to calculate age.
  • Use Add Person to include more people.
  • View each person's detailed age and birthday information.
  • Select any two people in the Age Difference section to compare their ages.

Calculation Method

Age is calculated using calendar years, months and days. Total days are based on the actual calendar difference between the date of birth and the selected calculation date.

Age Difference is calculated as the calendar interval between the two dates of birth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate age as of a past date?
Yes. Select any date on or after the date of birth.

Can I compare more than two people?
Yes. Add multiple people and select any two for comparison.

Does the calculator support leap years?
Yes. Actual calendar dates, including leap years, are considered.

Can I save my results?
Yes. Use Print / Save as PDF to save your results.
