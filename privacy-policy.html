Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

At CalcConvert, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information associated with your use of our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used and the choices available to you when visiting our website.

1. Information We May Collect

The type of information collected may depend on how you use CalcConvert. This may include:

  • Contact information: If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, name and any information you choose to include in your message.
  • Technical information: Our hosting, security or service providers may process information such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system and website request details.
  • Usage information: If analytics services are enabled, information about visits, pages viewed, interactions and general website usage may be collected.

At present, CalcConvert does not require visitors to create an account or register to use its calculators and converters.

2. Calculator Information

Some CalcConvert tools allow users to enter values such as salary figures, amounts, dates or other calculation details. These values are used to generate the requested results.

The way information is processed may depend on how an individual tool operates. Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive, confidential or personally identifiable information into calculator fields.

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.
  • Understanding which calculators and tools are useful to visitors.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Displaying advertisements, where advertising services are enabled.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites or third-party services may store on your device. Similar technologies may also be used to support website functionality, understand usage or deliver advertisements.

CalcConvert may use essential cookies or similar technologies where needed for website operation. If analytics or advertising services are introduced, they may also use cookies or similar technologies for their respective purposes.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features or services.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with CalcConvert and to improve our website. When enabled, Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser details, referral sources and interactions.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to provide these services. Information collected through this service is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and settings.

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .
