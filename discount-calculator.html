About the Discount Calculator

This free Discount Calculator helps shoppers, retailers and businesses calculate discounts, savings and final sale prices. Simply enter the original price and discount percentage to see how much you save and how much you need to pay.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  • Enter the original price of the product.
  • Enter the discount percentage offered by the seller.
  • View the discount amount and final price automatically.
  • Use the reverse calculator to find the original price from a sale price.

Discount Calculation Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount
Original Price = Sale Price ÷ (1 − Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Discount Calculation Example

If a product costs 1,000 and has a 20% discount, the discount amount is 200. Subtracting 200 from the original price gives a final sale price of 800.

This calculator provides general mathematical calculations. Actual prices, taxes, delivery charges and other fees may vary by seller.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a Discount Calculator?

A Discount Calculator calculates the amount saved on a product and its final price after applying a discount percentage.

2. How do I calculate a discount amount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100.

3. How do I calculate the final price after discount?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price.

4. Can I calculate the original price from a sale price?

Yes. Enter the sale price and discount percentage in the Reverse Discount Calculator to find the original price.

5. Can I use this calculator for any currency?

Yes. The calculator uses numbers without a fixed currency symbol, so you can use it with any currency.
