How to Calculate Percentages

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. It is commonly used for discounts, price changes, marks, salaries, business calculations and everyday comparisons.

1. Find a Percentage of a Number

Formula: (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: 20% of 500 = 100.

2. Find What Percentage One Number Is of Another

Formula: (First Number ÷ Second Number) × 100

Example: 50 is 25% of 200.

3. Percentage Increase

Formula: ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: An increase from 100 to 120 is 20%.

4. Percentage Decrease

Formula: ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: A decrease from 200 to 150 is 25%.
