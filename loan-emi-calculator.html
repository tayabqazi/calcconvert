About Loan EMI Calculator

CalcConvert's free Loan EMI Calculator helps estimate monthly installments, interest or profit, down payments, upfront fees and overall financing cost. Use it for personal loans, vehicle financing, home mortgages, property financing and business loans.

Vehicle Financing and Home Mortgage

For vehicle or property financing, enter the purchase price and down payment. The estimated financed amount is calculated by subtracting the down payment from the purchase price. Other upfront payments, such as processing fees, registration and insurance, remain separate and are not automatically added to the loan.

Other Monthly Payments

You can enter additional monthly costs, such as monthly insurance or service charges. These are added to the monthly outflow estimate but remain separate from the loan EMI and amortization schedule.

Fixed Interest Rate

Under a fixed-rate arrangement, the annual interest or profit rate remains unchanged according to the loan agreement.

Benchmark Rate + Bank Spread

Some financing arrangements use a reference benchmark rate plus a bank spread or margin. The calculator suggests a benchmark based on the selected currency, but the user must enter the current applicable benchmark rate and bank spread.

Annual Rate = Benchmark Rate + Bank Spread
Example: 11.50% KIBOR + 1.00% spread = 12.50% annual rate.

EMI Calculation Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

P = Financed Loan Principal
r = Monthly Interest Rate
n = Total Number of Monthly Installments

Important Notes and Limitations

  • Results are estimates and may differ from actual bank repayment schedules.
  • Other upfront payments and additional monthly payments are displayed separately; they are not added to the loan principal.
  • The calculator assumes the entered annual rate remains unchanged for the full tenure.
  • Benchmark rates are not fetched live. Confirm the applicable benchmark and bank margin with your lender.
  • Actual bank calculations may use different day-count, compounding, fees or repayment conventions.
  • Islamic financing structures, balloon payments and special repayment arrangements may require a separate calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Installment. It is the regular monthly payment used to repay a loan, including principal and interest or profit.

2. Can I calculate car financing and home mortgage payments?

Yes. Select Vehicle / Car Financing, Home Loan / Mortgage or Property Financing, then enter the purchase price and down payment. The calculator estimates the financed amount automatically.

3. How is the loan amount calculated for vehicle or property financing?

The estimated loan amount is the purchase price minus the down payment. Other upfront payments are shown separately and are not automatically added to the loan.

4. What is the difference between fixed and floating rates?

A fixed rate remains unchanged according to the loan agreement. A floating rate can change when its benchmark rate is revised.

5. Does this calculator automatically update benchmark rates?

No. The calculator selects a suggested benchmark based on the chosen currency, but users must enter the current applicable benchmark rate and bank spread.

6. Are bank fees, insurance and other payments included?

You can enter other upfront payments and additional monthly payments separately. They are included in the estimated overall cost but are not added to the loan principal or EMI.
