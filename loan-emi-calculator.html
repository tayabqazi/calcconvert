About Loan EMI Calculator

The CalcConvert Loan EMI Calculator helps you estimate monthly loan installments, total interest or profit and total repayment. It supports fixed annual rates and benchmark-based financing rates.

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the regular monthly payment made by a borrower to repay a loan. Each installment normally includes a portion of the original loan amount and the applicable interest or profit.

Fixed Interest Rate

Under a fixed-rate arrangement, the annual interest or profit rate remains unchanged for the period specified in the loan agreement.

Benchmark Rate + Bank Spread

Some financing arrangements use a reference benchmark rate plus a bank spread or margin. The total annual rate is calculated by adding the benchmark rate and the agreed bank spread.

Annual Rate = Benchmark Rate + Bank Spread
Example: 11.50% KIBOR + 1.00% spread = 12.50% annual rate.

Different markets use different benchmarks, such as KIBOR, SOFR, SONIA, EURIBOR and TONA. The applicable benchmark and margin depend on the bank, product and loan agreement.

EMI Calculation Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

P = Loan Principal
r = Monthly Interest Rate
n = Total Number of Monthly Installments

Important Note About Floating Rates

For benchmark-based financing, this calculator uses the benchmark rate and spread entered by the user. It assumes that the resulting annual rate remains unchanged throughout the loan tenure. Actual installments may change when the bank revises its benchmark rate. The bank's repayment schedule and financing agreement should be treated as authoritative.

Calculator Limitations

  • Results are estimates and may differ from actual bank schedules.
  • Processing fees, insurance, taxes and other charges are not included.
  • Variable-rate changes are not automatically forecast.
  • Actual bank calculations may use different day-count or compounding conventions.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Installment. It is the regular monthly payment used to repay a loan.

2. What is the difference between fixed and floating rates?

A fixed rate remains unchanged according to the loan agreement. A floating rate can change when its benchmark rate is revised.

3. What is KIBOR + Bank Spread?

It means the applicable KIBOR benchmark rate plus the additional percentage margin charged by the bank. For example, 11.50% KIBOR plus 1% spread results in a 12.50% annual rate.

4. Does this calculator automatically update benchmark rates?

No. Users must enter the latest benchmark rate and bank spread provided by their financial institution.

5. Does the calculator include bank fees and insurance?

No. The calculation covers the loan principal and entered interest or profit rate. Additional charges must be considered separately.

6. Can I use this calculator for Islamic financing?

You can enter the applicable annual profit or markup rate to estimate installments. Actual Islamic financing structures and payment schedules may differ.
