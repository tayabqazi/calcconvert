About Salary Increment Calculator

The CalcConvert Salary Increment Calculator is a free online tool designed to help employees, HR professionals and employers calculate revised salaries after a salary increase.

You can calculate an increment using either a percentage or a fixed amount. The calculator also allows you to apply the increment to basic salary or gross salary and view the resulting monthly and annual salary figures.

How to Use the Salary Increment Calculator

  • Enter your current basic salary.
  • Enter your current gross salary, including allowances, if available.
  • Select whether the increment will apply to basic salary or gross salary.
  • Choose Percentage (%) or Fixed Amount as the increment type.
  • Enter the increment percentage or amount.
  • Click Calculate Increment to view your revised salary and comparison.

Salary Increment Calculation Formula

The calculator uses the following formulas to calculate the monthly and annual salary increase.

Percentage Increment:
Increment Amount = Salary × Increment Percentage ÷ 100
Fixed Amount Increment:
Increment Amount = Fixed Increment Value
Revised Gross Salary:
Revised Gross Salary = Current Gross Salary + Monthly Increment
Annual Increment:
Annual Increment = Monthly Increment × 12
Revised Annual Gross Salary:
Revised Annual Gross Salary = Revised Monthly Gross Salary × 12

Example of Salary Increment Calculation

Suppose your current basic salary is 60,000 and your gross salary is 100,000. If your employer gives you a 15% increment on basic salary, the calculation will be:

Basic Salary vs Gross Salary Increment

A basic salary increment increases the basic pay component. In this calculator, the same increment amount is also added to gross salary, while other allowances remain unchanged.

A gross salary increment is calculated using the complete gross salary, including allowances. The basic salary remains unchanged when the increment is applied to gross salary.

Important Notes

  • Enter salary amounts in your preferred currency.
  • If gross salary is not entered, the calculator uses basic salary as gross salary.
  • When increment is applied to basic salary, the same increment amount is added to gross salary.
  • When increment is applied to gross salary, basic salary remains unchanged.
  • Other allowances and deductions are not separately recalculated.
  • This calculator does not calculate income tax, provident fund or other payroll deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I calculate my salary increment percentage?

Multiply your current salary by the increment percentage and divide the result by 100. For example, a 10% increment on 50,000 equals 5,000 per month.

2. Can I calculate a fixed salary increment?

Yes. Select Fixed Amount as the increment type and enter the amount you want to add to your current salary.

3. What is the difference between basic and gross salary increment?

A basic salary increment is calculated on basic pay, whereas a gross salary increment is calculated on total gross salary, including allowances.

4. How is annual salary increment calculated?

Annual increment is calculated by multiplying the monthly increment amount by 12, assuming the revised salary applies for all 12 months.

5. Does this calculator calculate income tax and deductions?

No. This calculator estimates salary increases before tax and other deductions. It does not calculate income tax, provident fund, social security or other payroll deductions.

6. Can I save or print my salary increment results?

Yes. After calculating your revised salary, click Print / Save as PDF to print the results or save them as a PDF document.

7. Is this Salary Increment Calculator free?

Yes. CalcConvert provides this salary increment calculator free of charge, without requiring user registration.
