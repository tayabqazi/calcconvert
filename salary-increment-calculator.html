About Salary Increment Calculator

This free calculator helps employees and employers estimate revised salaries after a percentage-based or fixed-amount increment. It supports increments on either basic salary or gross salary.

Note: If increment is applied to basic salary, gross salary is increased by the same amount. Other allowances are assumed unchanged. This is an estimate and does not calculate tax or deductions.
