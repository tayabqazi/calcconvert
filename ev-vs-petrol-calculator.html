How Does the EV vs Petrol Running Cost Calculator Work?

This calculator compares the cost of petrol and electricity required to travel the same distance. Enter the prices and consumption figures that apply to your vehicle and location.

Petrol Vehicle Running Cost

Petrol Cost per KM = Petrol Price per Litre ÷ Petrol Mileage (km per litre)

Electric Vehicle Running Cost

EV Cost per KM = Electricity Rate per kWh × EV Consumption (kWh per 100 km) ÷ 100

Monthly and Yearly Cost

Daily Cost = Cost per KM × Daily Driving Distance
Monthly Cost = Daily Cost × Driving Days per Month
Yearly Cost = Monthly Cost × 12

Frequently Asked Questions

What information do I need?

You need the petrol price per litre, petrol vehicle mileage in km per litre, electricity price per kWh, EV consumption in kWh per 100 km and your daily driving distance.

Is electricity measured in kW or kWh?

Electricity consumption and electricity tariffs are entered in kWh (kilowatt-hour), commonly called a unit. kW measures power, not the amount of energy consumed.

Does the result include maintenance or vehicle purchase cost?

No. This calculator compares petrol and electricity running costs only. It does not include maintenance, insurance, vehicle purchase price, financing, charging equipment or battery replacement.

Are the savings guaranteed?

No. Results are estimates based on the prices, vehicle efficiency and driving distance you enter. Actual costs may vary due to driving conditions, charging losses, traffic, fuel prices and electricity tariffs.
