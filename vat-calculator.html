About VAT

VAT stands for Value Added Tax. It is a consumption tax applied to goods and services in many countries. VAT rates and rules vary by country and transaction type.

This calculator allows you to enter your own VAT rate. It is a general calculation tool and does not determine which tax rate applies to a particular transaction.

How to Use the VAT Calculator

  • Select Add VAT to calculate VAT on a price before tax.
  • Select Remove VAT to extract VAT from a VAT-inclusive price.
  • Enter the price amount.
  • Enter the applicable VAT rate as a percentage.
  • Optionally enter a currency code or symbol.
  • Click Calculate VAT to view the breakdown.

VAT Calculation Formulas

Add VAT:

VAT Amount = Net Price × (VAT Rate ÷ 100)
Gross Price = Net Price + VAT Amount

Remove VAT:

Net Price = Gross Price ÷ (1 + VAT Rate ÷ 100)
VAT Amount = Gross Price − Net Price

Calculation Examples

If the price before VAT is 100 and the VAT rate is 15%:

VAT Amount = 100 × 15% = 15
Total Price = 100 + 15 = 115

If the price including 15% VAT is 115:

Price Before VAT = 115 ÷ 1.15 = 100
VAT Amount = 115 − 100 = 15

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Add VAT and Remove VAT?
Add VAT calculates tax on a price before VAT. Remove VAT extracts the tax included in the final price.

Can I use this calculator for different countries?
Yes. Enter the VAT rate applicable to your country. The calculator does not automatically select country-specific tax rates.

Does this calculator support different currencies?
Yes. You can enter an optional currency code or symbol. The calculator does not perform currency conversion.

Is VAT always charged at the same rate?
No. Rates, exemptions and applicable rules differ between countries, products and services. Verify the correct rate before using the result for tax reporting.
