How to Use the Business Profit Calculator

Select your business type, currency and reporting period. Enter revenue, returns or discounts, direct costs, operating expenses, other income and other expenses. Add an optional tax rate or fixed tax amount to estimate net profit after tax.

Profit Calculation Formula

Net Revenue = Total Revenue − Sales Returns & Discounts

Gross Profit = Net Revenue − Direct Costs

Operating Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses

Profit Before Tax = Operating Profit + Other Income − Finance Cost − Other Expenses

Net Profit / Loss = Profit Before Tax − Tax

What Are Direct Costs?

Direct costs are expenses directly related to the goods or services sold. Examples include the cost of goods sold, project materials, production labour, subcontractors, packaging and direct delivery. The relevant costs depend on the type of business.

Inventory and Purchases

For trading, retail and manufacturing businesses, only the cost attributable to goods sold is deducted in this profit calculation. Unsold inventory is not treated as an expense in the period.

Profit Margin vs. Markup

Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of net revenue. Markup measures gross profit as a percentage of direct costs. They are different measures and should not be confused.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this calculator for different types of businesses?

Yes. It is designed for trading, freelance, service, manufacturing, e-commerce, retail, construction and other businesses.

Does the calculator automatically calculate income tax?

No. You can enter a tax percentage or fixed amount to estimate tax. Actual tax depends on local laws, business structure and applicable rules.

Does inventory purchase reduce profit immediately?

No. For this calculation, enter the cost of goods sold during the selected period. Unsold inventory is excluded from direct costs.

Can I save or print my results?

Yes. Use Print / Save as PDF after calculating your results.
